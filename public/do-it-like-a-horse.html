do it like a horse

~ 2026-05-24

During christmas 2024 I got sick and I was bored, so I practiced how to cough like a horse.

It's a good exercise in breath control and it's less aggressive than a normal cough, so it doesn't strain the throat as much.

Now I can even sneeze like a horse.
